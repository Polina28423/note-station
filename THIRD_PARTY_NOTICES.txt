Original license notices are retained in `ui/vendor/tinymce/license.txt` and
`ui/vendor/dompurify/LICENSE`. Distribution of the app with TinyMCE is subject to
its GPL terms unless a commercial license is obtained. The Rust and UI source
for this prototype is present in this workspace; do not remove dependency notices.

Packages were downloaded from the official npm registry. Their tarball SHA-512
digests were checked against the registry's `dist.integrity` metadata before extraction.
Only the editor runtime, selected plugins, skins and license files are included.

Editor.js, selected tools and their runtime dependencies are bundled locally in
`ui/vendor/editorjs/editorjs.bundle.js`; parse5 and entities are in `html-parser.js`.
Their license texts, including transitive packages, are retained in
`ui/vendor/editorjs/LICENSES.txt` and generated `*.LEGAL.txt` files.
Some npm packages omit LICENSE files: supplemental upstream license copies and
source URLs/SHA-256 are retained under `tools/editor-build/licenses/`.
Pinned versions and npm tarball integrity hashes are recorded in
`tools/editor-build/package-lock.json`. Installation uses `npm ci --ignore-scripts`.
Build tooling esbuild and test tooling Playwright are development dependencies;
they are not shipped in the app. TinyMCE remains available as the classic fallback,
so its licensing requirements still apply to this distribution.
